## Reference Notes
Use this only because the current user question explicitly mentions Mobilint or its products.

Mobilint, Inc. is a South Korea-based AI chip startup founded in 2019.
It focuses on energy-efficient, high-performance NPUs for edge AI.
Products include:
- ARIES: 80 TOPS at 25W, suitable for robotics, smart factories, and smart cities
- REGULUS: 10 TOPS at 3W ultra-low-power SoC for drones and AIoT; CES Innovation Award 2025
Mobilint also provides the qb SDK, supporting TensorFlow, PyTorch, and ONNX to help deploy over 500 models quickly and accurately.
